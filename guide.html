📖 Owner's guide

Up and running
in ten minutes.

Everything you need to set up Knockoff, run your first week of timesheets, and get your crew's hours into payroll. Come back any time — this page isn't going anywhere.

01

Sign in & create your business

Head to app.knockoff.co.nz/owner.html and enter your email. We'll email you a magic link — click it and you're in. No password to create or remember.

The first time you sign in, Knockoff sets up your business and starts your 7-day free trial automatically. From there, a quick 5-step setup wizard walks you through the basics below.

Tip: The magic-link email can take a minute and occasionally lands in spam. If it hasn't shown up, check your junk folder before requesting another.
02

Add your crew & set PINs

In the setup wizard (or later from your dashboard), add each crew member by name and give them a 4–6 digit PIN. That PIN is how they'll log in on their phone — so pick something they'll remember, and keep them all different.

  • Add everyone who fills out a timesheet — including yourself if you're on the tools.
  • You can add, rename, or remove crew any time as your team changes.
  • Your plan sets how many crew you can have: Starter up to 5, Pro up to 10, Business up to 20.
03

Add your job sites

Add the job sites your crew work across — a name for each is all you need (e.g. "12 Kauri Rd" or "Westfield fit-out"). When your crew submit a timesheet, they pick which site they were on, so you always know who was where.

Tip: Keep site names short and recognisable. You can add new sites whenever a job kicks off and archive old ones when they wrap up.
04

Share the clock-in link with your crew

Your crew don't need to download anything. Send them the crew clock-in link (Knockoff gives you a shareable link in the wizard and on your dashboard) — they open it on their phone, tap their name, enter their PIN, and they're in.

Get them to bookmark it or add it to their home screen so it's one tap every morning. WhatsApp or text is the easiest way to send it round the crew.

05

Approve hours each week

As your crew submit their days, entries land on your admin board in real time. Breaks are already deducted, so paid hours are worked out for you. Your job is just to check and approve.

  • Review entries individually, or approve everything pending in one hit.
  • Spot something off? Edit the entry before you approve it.
  • Nothing goes to payroll until you've approved it — you're always in control.
06

Export to your payroll

Once the week's hours are approved, click Export. Knockoff gives you a CSV that opens right in Excel — formatted ready for Smartly, and works with Xero, MYOB or any other payroll system. Because it opens in Excel, you can check the numbers before you commit them to payroll.

Using a different payroll provider? The export is a standard CSV. Email hello@knockoff.co.nz and we'll help you line it up.
07

Manage billing, plans & cancelling

When your trial ends, pick the plan that fits your crew size and add your card. Payments are handled securely by Stripe. Prices are GST-exclusive and 15% GST is added at checkout.

From the billing portal in your dashboard you can:

  • View and download your invoices and GST receipts.
  • Update your card details.
  • Move up or down a plan as your crew changes.
  • Cancel any time — your access runs to the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

No lock-in contracts, ever. Questions about a charge? Email hello@knockoff.co.nz and we'll sort it.
