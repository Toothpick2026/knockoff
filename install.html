📱 For the crew

Put Knockoff
on your phone.

Takes about thirty seconds. After this, Knockoff sits on your home screen like any other app — tap it, punch in your PIN, fill in your day. No App Store, no download, nothing to update.

01

Open the link in Safari

Your boss will have sent you a link. If you tapped it straight from WhatsApp, Messenger or your texts, it's opened inside that app's own little browser — and that one can't save Knockoff to your home screen.

Look at the bottom of the screen for the compass icon, or tap the ••• in the corner and choose Open in Safari.

This is the step everyone misses. If the next step doesn't look right, you're still in WhatsApp's browser rather than Safari. Come back and do this one first.
02

Tap the share button

It's the square with an arrow pointing up out of it, at the bottom of the Safari screen. On an older iPhone or an iPad it might be up the top right instead.

03

Choose Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the grey list that slides up. Add to Home Screen is a fair way down, past Copy and Print. Tap it, then tap Add in the top right.

Tip: You can rename it before you hit Add. Leave it as Knockoff so it's easy to spot when you're half awake.
04

You're done — check it works

The yellow Knockoff icon is now sitting on your home screen with the rest of your apps. Tap it, find your name in the list, and enter the 4–6 digit PIN your boss gave you.

If it asks to use your location, tap Allow While Using App. That's how the job site gets recorded when you clock in.

01

Open the link in Chrome

Your boss will have sent you a link. If you tapped it from WhatsApp or your texts it may have opened in that app's own browser. Tap the ⋮ in the corner and choose Open in browser or Open in Chrome.

02

Tap the three dots

Top right of the Chrome screen — three dots stacked up, ⋮. That opens Chrome's menu.

Even quicker: Chrome often pops up an Install app bar along the bottom by itself. If you see it, just tap that and skip ahead.
03

Choose Install app

Look for Install app or Add to Home screen — Samsung and other phones word it slightly differently. Tap it, then tap Install or Add to confirm.

04

You're done — check it works

The yellow Knockoff icon is now in your apps. Tap it, find your name in the list, and enter the 4–6 digit PIN your boss gave you.

If it asks to use your location, tap While using the app. That's how the job site gets recorded when you clock in.

When it plays up.

Four things go wrong. All four have a quick fix.

There's no Add to Home Screen in the list

You're in WhatsApp's browser, not Safari. Tap the compass icon at the bottom of the screen, or ••• then Open in Safari, and start again from there. On Android, the same thing happens if you're not in Chrome.

My name isn't in the list

Your boss hasn't added you yet, or has added you under a different spelling. Give them a nudge — it takes them about ten seconds from their dashboard.

I've forgotten my PIN

Your boss can see it and reset it from their dashboard. We can't look it up for you, so don't email us — ask them.

I'm out at a job with no signal

Fill your hours in when you're back in range. Nothing is lost — the entry only goes through when you hit Submit and your phone has coverage.

Sending this to your crew?

Copy this into your crew's group chat with your clock-in link on the end. Works better than explaining it seven times.
