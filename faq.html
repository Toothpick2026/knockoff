Pricing & billing
How much does Knockoff cost?

There are three plans, priced per month and based on your crew size:

  • Starter — $35/month — up to 5 crew
  • Pro — $55/month — up to 10 crew
  • Business — $95/month — up to 20 crew

All prices are in NZD and exclude GST. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial — no credit card needed to begin.

Is GST included in those prices?

No — the plan prices are GST exclusive. New Zealand GST of 15% is added at checkout. So, for example, the Starter plan is $35 + $5.25 GST = $40.25 per month in total.

Your Stripe receipt and tax invoice show the GST component separately, so it's ready for your accountant.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

When you sign up, you get 7 days free with no credit card required. You can add your crew, set up job sites, and run real timesheets during the trial — it's the full app, not a stripped-back demo.

When you're ready to keep going, you pick a plan and enter your card. If Knockoff isn't for you, just walk away — nothing is charged.

What happens when my crew grows past my plan's limit?

Each plan covers a crew-size band: Starter up to 5, Pro up to 10, Business up to 20. When your team grows, you simply move up to the next plan. If you need more than 20 crew, get in touch at hello@knockoff.co.nz and we'll sort you out.

How do I pay, and is it secure?

Payments are handled by Stripe, one of the world's most trusted payment processors. Knockoff never sees or stores your full card number — that stays with Stripe. You'll be charged automatically each month until you cancel.

Using Knockoff
Do my crew need to download an app?

No. Knockoff runs in the phone's web browser — your crew just open a link you share with them and bookmark it to their home screen. No App Store, no Play Store, no updates to chase.

How do crew log in?

Simple as it gets: your crew pick their name from a list and enter a 4–6 digit PIN you set for them. No email, no password to forget. If they can tap in a phone code, they can clock their hours.

Does it handle breaks and lunch deductions?

Yes. Breaks are pre-filled on each entry, and paid hours are calculated automatically once lunch is deducted — so you're not sitting there doing the maths for every person, every day. There's also a break reminder built in so crew don't run over.

How does payroll export work? Which payroll systems does it support?

Once you've approved your crew's hours, you click Export and Knockoff gives you a CSV that opens right in Excel — formatted ready for Smartly, and works with Xero, MYOB or any other payroll system. Because it opens in Excel, you can eyeball the numbers before you commit them to payroll.

Using a different payroll provider? Email us — it's a standard CSV and we're happy to help you line it up.

Can I approve or edit hours before they go to payroll?

Absolutely. Every entry your crew submits lands on your admin board for review. You can approve entries one at a time or all at once, and make corrections before anything is exported. Nothing goes to payroll without your say-so.

Cancelling & your data
How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time from the billing portal inside your owner dashboard — no phone calls, no awkward retention chat. Your subscription stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again after that.

There are no lock-in contracts. Cancel whenever it suits you.

Do I get a refund if I cancel mid-month?

When you cancel, your plan runs to the end of the current billing period, so you keep access to what you've paid for. We don't automatically pro-rate part-months, but if something's gone wrong or you've been charged in error, email hello@knockoff.co.nz and we'll make it right. Your rights under the NZ Consumer Guarantees Act always apply.

How is my data handled? Is it private?

Your business and crew data belongs to you. We handle personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Data is stored securely with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and payments are processed by Stripe. We never sell your data.

Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Is Knockoff actually a New Zealand product?

Yes — Knockoff is built and run in New Zealand by Corpworks Limited, for NZ trades. It's designed around NZ payroll (a CSV export ready for Smartly and usable with Xero, MYOB and any system) and GST, by people who've done the payroll-night grind themselves.
