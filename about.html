Knockoff is a Kiwi timesheet app built by people who've done payroll night the hard way — and decided there had to be a better way.

Who's behind it

Knockoff is built and run by Corpworks Limited, a New Zealand company. We're not a Silicon Valley startup that's never held a drill. We come from the trade world — the vans, the sites, the Friday afternoon scramble to work out who did what hours where.

That's exactly the problem Knockoff was built to kill. We got tired of chasing crew over WhatsApp, deciphering water-stained paper timesheets, and hand-calculating lunch breaks for every person, every day. So we built the tool we wished we had.

Why we built Knockoff

Most timesheet software is built for big corporates with an HR department and a training budget. It's bloated, expensive, and needs a manual to operate. That's no good to a builder, sparky, or plumber running a crew of five off the seat of a ute.

Knockoff does one job and does it properly: your crew clock their hours from their phone, breaks get deducted automatically, and you approve and export straight to payroll. No app store downloads. No spreadsheets. No Friday night maths.

What we believe

Running a trade business is hard enough without software making it harder. Tools should save you time, not eat your evening. Pricing should be clear, not a maze. And the people building your software should actually understand the job you do.
